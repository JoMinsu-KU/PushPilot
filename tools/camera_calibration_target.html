PushPilot / Camera calibration

A camera-only calibration target.

The robot stays held. Show this screen to the existing camera. Hold each view for about one second, move through the image corners and centre, and tilt the tablet gently around both axes. Keep the complete checkerboard visible.

Use landscape orientation. Keep the display flat, with automatic brightness and screen colour filters disabled for this capture. Avoid reflections and touching the robot or car.

The pattern preserves square geometry automatically. No ruler or measured square size is required to estimate camera intrinsics.
