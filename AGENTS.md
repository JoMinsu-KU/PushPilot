- This directory is the independent application repository boundary for new PushPilot implementation. Keep it runnable and packageable without files outside this directory.
- Use English for source code, identifiers, comments, errors, logs, tests, frontend copy, accessibility text and development documentation. Explain work to the user in Korean unless requested otherwise.
- The user selected Pretendard for all product typography. Use the self-hosted, unmodified Pretendard Variable v1.3.9 WOFF2 in `frontend/public/fonts/pretendard/`, with system sans-serif fallbacks and no CDN request. Preserve its bundled `OFL.txt`, source/version/hash record and copyright notice in source and built distributions. Keep machine-readable JSON/evidence in monospace. Do not change the font binary or substitute a system-only stack without a user request.
- Preserve the existing parent project, POC sources, handoff archives and historical evidence. A new repository does not make inherited work newly authored. Record any future import with its source/version, rights and exact changes in `CONTRIBUTIONS.md` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- Product direction follows the user's industrial light-dashboard references, superseding the earlier navy/cyan interface and the first flat light-card implementation: a dominant continuous spatial/tabletop scene, large rounded translucent information overlays, charcoal typography, diffuse shadows, a narrow full-height icon rail, a compact header and restrained orange interaction accents. Mission and compact summaries float over the scene; do not append them as a long page below it. The user requires no vertical scrolling in Workspace, Plan, Replay or dialogs; horizontal paging/carousels are allowed with explicit navigation. Prioritize landscape tablet layouts at 1194 × 834, 1366 × 1024 and 1024 × 768 CSS pixels. Follow `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`; it records the requested direction, not user design approval. Preserve Observe → Plan → Approve → Act → Verify and readable camera overlays. AR glasses remain a clearly labelled future companion concept. Avoid toy-like decorative props, invented factory metrics or connected hardware/voice/spatial registration claims.
- The scene spans the full application grid beneath the rail, header and mission/status cards; do not reserve a blank right scene column behind the cards. Use actual backdrop glass: approximately 59% → 45% white fill for mission/status surfaces, 38% for the rail and 45% for the header, with 12 px backdrop blur/105% saturation and darker operational text. Material effects apply only to overlays. Keep the camera image and scene unfiltered, with no image opacity, canvas recompression or board resampling. Preserve the same original image URL and SVG coordinate geometry. Inspect route start/goal/turn visibility; translucent cards still partly cover the image and do not guarantee full visibility or accessibility conformance.
- Camera is the default scene view. Preserve its original perspective, image pixels, aspect ratio and overlay geometry; do not rectify or resample the board. Spatial map remains a separate drawing illustration. Board focus may mask the surrounding desk/floor to a neutral tabletop only while map/version/hash/frame/view registration checks pass, with Full frame always available. Missing, mismatched or changed registration must withhold the mask and fall back to the complete Camera frame. Keep original image bytes, frame IDs and hashes for MUSE input, action review and immutable evidence. The display mask adds no source detail, removes no occlusion and establishes no metric accuracy, car pose, camera-to-BASE registration or physical motion authority.
- Voice goal input is part of the planned initial demo: bounded push-to-talk audio goes through the backend Meta Voice adapter, then editable original-language text, MUSE interpretation and explicit tablet plan approval. Keep typed input available. Provider credentials remain backend-only; transcripts never grant motion authority. Streaming and TTS are later options. See `docs/VOICE_INPUT.md` and `docs/SERVICE_COMPOSITION.md`; voice capture and transcription are not yet implemented or verified.
- Accept new typed/spoken goals only during non-executing preparation, never during an active mission or signal/review wait. A replacement requires explicit completion/termination and reconciliation. Plan trusted target-device HTTPS for microphone capture, and validate/normalize bounded recordings to Meta's supported mono 16-bit PCM WAV format before dispatch.
- The confirmed mission starts at symbolic PIZZA and pushes a wheel-less car on the surface toward a selected destination. Do not pick up or carry the car. Pushing and contact turning require later physical qualification.
- At every configured intersection, stop before the stop line even if the signal is green. After verified stopping, RED means wait; unknown, stale or obscured observations also inhibit entry. Fresh GREEN plus accepted local and MUSE checks permits automatic entry. Complete the qualified admitted crossing before the next approach. In-flight red interruption is outside the current scenario.
- Operator-switched red/green paper signals need a local observation/inhibition path separate from slower model review. Never describe web HOLD or terminating a server as immediate robot stopping.
